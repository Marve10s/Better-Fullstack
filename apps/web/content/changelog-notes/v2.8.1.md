### Libraries for PHP, Ruby, Crystal, and Elixir

Better Fullstack 2.8.1 fills in the PHP, Ruby, and Crystal ecosystems with framework, database, auth, and service choices, and adds five Elixir categories. Every option generates working code in the project, and the builder, CLI, and MCP server reject combinations that cannot work together.

### PHP

- **Frameworks:** Laravel and Symfony join Slim.
- **Data and auth:** Eloquent, Doctrine, or Cycle on SQLite. Bearer-token auth with Firebase JWT, lcobucci/jwt, or Symfony Security.
- **Services:** PHPUnit, Pest, or Codeception tests; Symfony Validator, Respect\Validation, or Laravel validation; Symfony Cache, Laminas Cache, Predis, or Laravel Cache; Symfony Messenger, Enqueue, or Laravel Queue jobs; Symfony Mailer, PHPMailer, or Laravel Mail; Monolog logging matched to the framework; and Guzzle, Symfony HttpClient, or a PHP-HTTP cURL client.

### Ruby

- **Frameworks:** Rails, Roda, and Grape join Sinatra.
- **Data and auth:** Active Record, Sequel, or ROM on SQLite. JWT, OmniAuth OIDC, or Rodauth sign-in.
- **Services:** Minitest, RSpec, or Test::Unit; dry-validation, Active Model, or JSONSchemer; Redis, Memcached, or in-process caching; Sidekiq, Resque, or Solid Queue jobs; Mail, Action Mailer, or Resend email; Semantic Logger, Ougai, or Lograge logging; and Faraday, HTTParty, or HTTPX clients.

### Crystal

- **Frameworks:** Lucky, Athena, and Marten join Kemal.
- **Data and auth:** crystal-db, Avram, or Marten ORM. JWT, Kemal sessions, or Marten Auth.
- **Services:** Spec, Spectator, or spec-kemal tests; Athena Validator, ActiveModel, or Marten Schema validation; memory, file, or Redis caching; and Sidekiq.cr, Mosquito, or Tasker jobs.

### Elixir

- **New categories:** LoggerJSON, LoggerFileBackend, or Logster logging; Dotenvy, Skogsra, or Confex runtime config; AMQP, Gnat, or brod messaging for RabbitMQ, NATS, or Kafka; EEx, Solid, or bbmustache templates; and OptionParser, Optimus, or Owl command-line tasks.
- **More choices:** Exq jobs, Peri and Tarams validation, built-in JSON and Jiffy, Linguist and CLDR Messages, and DNSCluster.
